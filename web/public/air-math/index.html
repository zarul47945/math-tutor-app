⚡ How scoring works

Correct answers earn 100 base points, up to 100 speed points in the first 10 seconds, and 10 × your streak (up to 50). Points catch fire at a 3-answer streak and go out on a miss. Wrong answers deduct 20 points and reset your streak. No timeouts.
